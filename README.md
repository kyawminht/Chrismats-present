# 🎄 Christmas 2025 – Personalized Digital Card

> A budget-friendly, heartfelt Christmas gift for colleagues across borders

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-000000?style=for-the-badge&logo=vercel&logoColor=white)](https://chrismats-present.vercel.app/gift-for/friends)

---

## 🎯 The Problem

I wanted to give Christmas gifts to my colleagues and boss in another country, but:
- 💸 Couldn't afford expensive presents
- 🌏 They live far away
- ⏰ Needed something personal and on-time

---

## ✨ The Solution

A **personalized digital Christmas card** with:

- 🎵 Christmas songs
- 🎊 Confetti animation
- ❄️ Snowfall effect
- 🔗 **Unique URLs** for each person with custom messages

| Person | Custom URL | Message |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Boss | `/yo` | Special appreciation message |
| Manager | `/manager` | Thank you for leadership |
| Colleagues | `/colleague-1`, `/colleague-2` | "You're in my thoughts" |


## 🏆 Why I'm Proud

> *"I couldn't buy expensive gifts. So I built something meaningful instead. They received it on time. They felt appreciated. That's worth more than money."*
